# LGPD and Document Digitization: Practical Compliance Notes

Digitizing documents almost always means processing personal data.

Note: this is **informational** and not legal advice. Validate decisions with your legal counsel / DPO.

## What to get right early

### 1) Legal basis and purpose

Record, per document category:

- legal basis
- purpose
- retention period
- who can access

### 2) Data minimization

Extract/store only what you need to achieve the purpose.

### 3) Retention and deletion

Define retention periods and implement deletion or archival.

### 4) Security controls

- encryption at rest/in transit
- least-privilege access
- secrets management
- audit logs

### 5) Incident response

Have a basic playbook: detection → containment → notification → remediation.

---

If you’re implementing OCR in a system, prefer an approach that supports job tracking/logs so you can audit processing.
