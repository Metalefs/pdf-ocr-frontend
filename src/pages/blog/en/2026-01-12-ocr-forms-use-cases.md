# 5 OCR Use Cases for Business Forms

Business forms combine predictable structure with recurring volume — which makes them a great fit for OCR automation.

Note: any “results” depend on volume, scan quality, and review time. Treat ROI numbers as scenario-based, not universal.

## 1) Contracts and legal intake

- make scans searchable
- extract key fields (parties, dates, values)
- keep a human review step for exceptions

## 2) Invoices and accounting

- OCR + data extraction
- integrate with ERP/accounting systems
- validate totals and tax IDs

## 3) HR documents

- onboarding packets
- signed forms
- retention policies matter here

## 4) Compliance and audits

- searchable PDFs accelerate audits
- ensure access controls and audit logs

## 5) Customer intake forms

- route submissions to the right team
- auto-create CRM tickets
- notify via email/Slack/webhook
